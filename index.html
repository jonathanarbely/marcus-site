Marcus Aurelius, Read AloudSupport

A free app for iPhone and iPad with the Meditations of Marcus Aurelius, one passage a day: painted pages, read aloud in English or German, the words lighting up as they are read.

Questions and feedback

Write to jonathan@bymagellan.co, or use Send feedback in the app's Settings. Corrections to the translation are especially welcome; please name the passage (for example 2.1).

Good to know

  • One passage a day. Each of the twelve books is a map with one stop per passage. Read on as far as you like; one passage a day keeps your streak.
  • English or German. The whole app follows the language you choose; change it any time in Settings.
  • The translation. The English and the German are new, made with AI tools from the Greek and checked against it before they are published. The narration is an AI voice.
  • iPhone and iPad. With iCloud turned on, your progress follows you between your own devices.
  • Privacy. No account, no ads, no tracking. See the privacy policy.
